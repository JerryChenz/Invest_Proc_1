## Framework

### Country/Region Analysis
- Use PEST analysis

### Sector Analysis
1. **了解行业现状 (Understand the Current Industry Situation)**
   - 5W2H方法 (5W2H Method)
   - 产业链图 (Industry Chain Diagram)
   - 产业分赃链图 (Industry Profit Distribution Chain Diagram)
   - 市场规模预估法实现 (Market Size Estimation Method Implementation)
2. **分析行业动能 (Analyze Industry Dynamics)**
   - 外部动能 (External Dynamics)
   - 内部动能 (Internal Dynamics)
   - 竞争壁垒 (Competitive Barriers)
   - 行业周期 (Industry Cycle)
3. **推测行业变化 (Predict Industry Changes)**
   - 竞品分析 (Competitor Analysis)
4. **找出行业带给自身的机会 (Identify Opportunities for Oneself from the Industry)**
   - SWOT

### Company Analysis
- Porter's Five Forces
- 商业模式画布 (Business Model Canvas)
